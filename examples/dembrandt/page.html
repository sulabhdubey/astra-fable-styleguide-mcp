Follow the declared color.

This self-contained fixture declares --accent, --surface and --ink. The token checker compares their computed values with a pinned Dembrandt export.

Edit --accent to #ef4444 in a copy, then recheck to see a mismatch. Remove its declaration to see an incomplete check.

The white button text and gray code background are observed colors. They have no declared custom property in this fixture.
